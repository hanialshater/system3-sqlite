# SQLite in Clojure: art direction

This company's art follows the city's shared style, `system3-city ART.md` (true isometric, light from the upper left,
three tones per face from the city's palette, labels on surfaces, original logos, subtle motion that is safe under
reduced motion). This file keeps what is particular to SQLite in Clojure. The city's illustration draws it in system3-meta
`hq/city-scene.js` (`hq/ART.md`).

## The metaphor

**A library of tables**: a warehouse whose face is ruled like a table, with crates of rows stacked outside.

## Its building in the city

- **The building:** a sand-coloured warehouse with a flat slate roof and skylights, its front face ruled into rows and columns, a wooden door, and gridded crates beside it.
- **Live data it shows:** a crane while a milestone is active; people at its door for the members working in the last hour.
- **The roof sign:** its logo, on a light board, lit at night.

## Accent colours

- **Accent:** sand `#c9a46a`; slate for the roof.
- **Status colours:** the city's reserved status colours only (running, ended, onboarding, blocked).

## The logo

`company/logo.svg`: a feather whose barbs are nested parentheses: a light engine, written in a Lisp. Original: not SQLite's feather and not Clojure's mark.

## Its HQ pages and products

Its HQ pages use the HQ's own tokens (light and dark, phone width 390) and the city's page templates; nothing on a page imitates the illustration.
