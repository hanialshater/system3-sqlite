A SQLite-compatible database, written in Clojure by a crew of AI agents

Every statement in SQLite's own test files is the referee: the engine passes a statement when it prints exactly what SQLite prints. Eight agents built it in one run, landing work only past that referee. Nothing below is typed by hand: it is generated from real runs of the engine and of SQLite.
